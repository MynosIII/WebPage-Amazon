Revolution: connecting Amazon catalog, advertising and content

How PPC, automotive fitment, A+ content and channel coordination connect in my work at Revolution Supply.

Matías Gaglio · 08/11/2026

Leer en español

Looking at the whole channel

Since June 2025, I have worked on Revolution Supply’s Amazon channel in automotive products. My work connects catalog management, advertising, content and commercial analysis. Investment decisions need context: the advertised product, whether buyers understand fitment and whether the offer can support demand.

Fitment shapes the information

Lug nuts, bolts and wheel spacers require precise technical descriptions. Bolt pattern, thread, center bore, thickness and vehicle compatibility affect the purchase. I work on titles, bullets and catalog structure to make that information consistent, alongside Amazon fitment validation.

PPC starts with a business question

Campaign structure distinguishes queries, match types and product targeting. I read ROAS and ACoS alongside total sales, TACoS, conversion and profitability. These views support product prioritization and budget reviews; a single metric change needs context before it becomes a conclusion.

Content answers purchase questions

For A+ and galleries, I assign each asset a purpose: specifications, construction, fitment or comparison. Recent work includes explaining one-piece construction and giving the 72-hour salt spray test more visibility. Test evidence needs its scope preserved rather than being presented as an unlimited guarantee.

Coordination is part of the work

Catalog, operations and advertising depend on one another. Listing reviews, title changes and creative development need shared priorities and follow-up. My responsibilities include organizing the team, setting objectives and reviewing progress so that analysis leads to concrete action.

Connecting daily work to case studies

This article describes my overall approach. Individual portfolio cases cover advertising, content and analytics with their own evidence. Preserving that context helps explain my contribution without combining reporting periods, attributing every change to one action or disclosing internal operating figures.
