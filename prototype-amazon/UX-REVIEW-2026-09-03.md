# UX and media review — 3 September 2026

## Outcome

Published the corrected Amazon-inspired interface to https://matiasgaglio.vercel.app/. Original project copy and media order are preserved. Generated posters are frames from the original videos, not replacement artwork.

## Findings resolved

- Catalog cards initially displayed the generic social card while fetching their real media. They now show a stable loading state, prioritize the first results, cache media selection, and resolve more cards ahead of scrolling.
- Video previews required opening MP4 files of up to 87 MB. Nine lightweight JPEG posters now cover the original motion/conversion videos. Full conversion videos use a poster and load on demand inside their case pages.
- The catalog-recovery photograph was 3.53 MB at 5472 × 3648. Its same-frame 1600-pixel derivative is 303,944 bytes; the original asset remains available.
- Translated JavaScript selectors broke the Spanish conversion-video pages. Correct selectors restore all five original videos and four-part breakdowns without rewriting their data.
- Eight mobile overflows appeared across long case pages. Flexible source containers, wrapping headings and keyboard-accessible horizontal table regions now keep content inside the viewport without deleting columns or text.
- The old 404 used the previous visual system. The new bilingual 404 uses the shared marketplace header, search, theme control and recovery links, with a real HTTP 404 and noindex metadata.
- Added a brief branded entrance, media-ready transitions and restrained card interactions. Reduced-motion preferences disable the entrance and motion effects.
